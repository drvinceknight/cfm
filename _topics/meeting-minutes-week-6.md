This week's group meeting covers the following topics from the
[Python for Mathematics](https://vknight.org/pfm/) book:

- [Variables, conditional statements, and while loops]({{ site.baseurl }}/topics/variables-conditional-statements-and-while-loops.html)
- [Functions and data structures]({{ site.baseurl }}/topics/functions-and-data-structures.html)

## Example template

Download and print the Week 6 minutes form, or copy the structure into a
sheet of your own:

[**Week 6 minutes template (PDF)**]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/minutes/week-6.pdf)

The form covers attendance, what was discussed, any disagreements that
arose, questions for the contact hour, and action items for the coming
week. See the [Meetings]({{ site.baseurl }}/topics/meetings.html) topic
page for the format of meeting minutes in general and the recipe we use
for disagreements.

## Reminders

- The **chair** and the **secretary** for the meeting are different roles,
  and both rotate from week to week. The secretary writes the minutes
  during the meeting.
- This is your group's first meeting. An
  [example agenda]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/agendas/first-meeting.pdf)
  for the first meeting is available as a PDF.
- Submit the printed minutes as a group at the weekly contact hour.
