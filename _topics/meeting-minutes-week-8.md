This week you work through the following topics on your own, and each
member reports on them at the group meeting:

- [LaTeX and writing mathematics]({{ site.baseurl }}/topics/latex-and-writing-mathematics.html),
  worked through from the [LaTeX notes](https://vknight.org/tex/) and the
  two guides to writing mathematics linked on that page. The class hour
  this week is the subject librarian's session on researching sources,
  not a lecture on LaTeX, so the notes are yours to work through.
- [Modularisation of code]({{ site.baseurl }}/topics/modularisation-of-code.html),
  a chapter of [Python for Mathematics](https://vknight.org/pfm/).

The template for Week 8 adds a **project progress** section so the group
records what has been written, tested, or decided since the previous
meeting.
