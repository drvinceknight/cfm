This week you work through the following topics on your own, and each
member reports on them at the group meeting:

- [Presenting mathematics]({{ site.baseurl }}/topics/presenting-mathematics.html),
  worked through from the
  [principles of presentation](https://vknight.org/pop/). The class hour
  this week is spent watching presentations from past years, so the
  principles themselves are yours to work through.
- [Documentation of code]({{ site.baseurl }}/topics/documentation-of-code.html),
  a chapter of [Python for Mathematics](https://vknight.org/pfm/).

The template's **project progress** section is the place to record the
current state of the documentation written for the group library.
