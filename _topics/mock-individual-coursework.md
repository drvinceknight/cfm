individual coursework before the real one is released.

- [Mock coursework]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/assessment/mock/mock.ipynb)
- [Mock solutions]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/assessment/mock/mock_solutions.ipynb)

Attempt the mock before looking at the solutions. The marking criteria
are the same as for the individual coursework itself and are described
on the [Assessment]({{ site.baseurl }}/assessment/#individual-marking-criteria)
page: three marks per question for the analysis and two for the
communication.
