A small example of a full project

2025-04-04

On Friday I gave you a further example of all that is required for the software aspect of your project.

You can see a recording of the class here

In class I built a small library for Bayes' theorem. You can find each of the 3 files below:

  • bayes.py.
  • test_bayes.py.
  • README.md.

As I mentioned in the class there would be ways to extend the library (it is quite a minimalistic example so that I could do it in an hour).

Furthermore this would not be a complete submission for your project as you are also required to submit:

  • A recording of your presentation.
  • The source file and the pdf of your paper.
  • A contributions.md file.
