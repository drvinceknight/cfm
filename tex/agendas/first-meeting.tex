\section*{Purpose}

First meeting: introduce ourselves, agree how we will work together, and
surface initial ideas for the project.

\section*{Agenda}

\begin{enumerate}

  \item \textbf{Introductions} \hfill\textit{Inform, 5 minutes}\\
  Name, what you enjoy studying, and one thing you want from this module.

  \item \textbf{Week 6 content} \hfill\textit{Inform / Discuss, 20 minutes}\\
  Each member reports briefly on what they have read in:
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,topsep=0.1em,itemsep=0pt]
    \item Variables, conditional statements, and while loops.
    \item Functions and data structures.
  \end{itemize}
  Time is included for any questions a member would like to raise.

  \item \textbf{Rotation of chair and secretary} \hfill\textit{Decide, 3 minutes}\\
  Agree the rota for Weeks 6 to 10.

  \item \textbf{Communication outside meetings} \hfill\textit{Decide, 3 minutes}\\
  Agree the group's main channel (chat, email, or a shared document).

  \item \textbf{Meeting time for the rest of term} \hfill\textit{Decide, 4 minutes}\\
  Agree when and where to meet (must be before the contact hour).

  \item \textbf{Initial ideas for the group project} \hfill\textit{Discuss, 8 minutes}\\
  Each member brings one idea. No decision required at this meeting.

  \item \textbf{Action items} \hfill\textit{Decide, 2 minutes}\\
  Who does what before the next meeting?

\end{enumerate}
