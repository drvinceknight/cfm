Meeting minutes: Week 9

This week you work through the following topics on your own, and each member reports on them at the group meeting:

  • Presenting mathematics, worked through from the principles of presentation. The class hour this week is spent watching presentations from past years, so the principles themselves are yours to work through.
  • Documentation of code, a chapter of Python for Mathematics.

The template's project progress section is the place to record the current state of the documentation written for the group library.
