Submission

The group project is submitted this week.

Your submission is a set of 8 files: the paper source and PDF, the bibliography, the library, its tests, the README, the recording of your presentation, and the record of contributions. Each one is listed, with a description and a link to the relevant topic page, in the submission requirements section of the Assessment page.

Two things to check before you submit:

  • Every file is named as the requirements describe.
  • The paper compiles from the main.tex you are submitting, and the tests pass against the <library>.py you are submitting.
